Why Choose Dr. Moore

Diagnostic Precision Meets Clinical Depth

Not every chiropractor has the same training, tools, or clinical experience. Here is what sets this practice apart.

Mobile's Only Digital Motion X-Ray

DMX captures spinal movement in real time — revealing ligament instability and soft-tissue injuries that static imaging misses. Critical for accurate trauma documentation.

Fellowship-Trained Spine Specialist

The FPSC fellowship (Cleveland University / SUNY Jacobs) is a two-year post-doctoral program with advanced training in spinal trauma, imaging, biomechanics, and complex spinal conditions.

NIH-Published Research

Dr. Moore's research has been published through the National Institutes of Health, grounding his clinical approach in peer-reviewed evidence rather than opinion.

Certified Independent Chiropractic Examiner

The CICE credential reflects specialized training and examination in independent medical evaluation and AMA Guides impairment rating methodology.

Advanced Therapeutic Equipment

Duolith SD1 Ultra shockwave therapy, Aspen Class 4 laser, computerized spinal decompression, and ArthroStim instrumented adjusting — therapies backed by clinical evidence.

Forensic & Expert Witness Qualified

With CICE, MACIME, and CPIS credentials, Dr. Moore is qualified to provide expert opinions in injury cases — bringing the same clinical rigor to documentation as to treatment.

Clinical Services

Comprehensive Spinal & Injury Care

From acute trauma evaluation to long-term rehabilitation, our services are calibrated for complex, injury-related cases.

Diagnostic

Digital Motion X-Ray

Fluoroscopic imaging that captures spinal motion in real time — the only technology in Mobile, AL capable of visualizing ligament instability dynamically.

Regenerative

Shockwave Therapy

Duolith SD1 Ultra radial and focused shockwave — used for chronic tendinopathy, heel pain, myofascial trigger points, and calcific conditions.

Photobiomodulation

Class 4 Laser Therapy

Aspen high-powered laser therapy for deep tissue penetration — used to help manage pain and inflammation without medication.

Decompression

Spinal Decompression

Computerized intersegmental decompression for disc herniation, radiculopathy, and degenerative disc disease — a conservative, non-surgical option for appropriately selected patients.

Instrumented

ArthroStim Adjusting

High-velocity, low-amplitude mechanical adjusting instrument — precise neurological input without the manual thrust, ideal for post-trauma and sensitive patients.

Manual Therapy

Chiropractic Adjustments

Evidence-based spinal and extremity manipulation to restore joint motion, reduce pain, and normalize neurological function following injury or chronic dysfunction.

Soft Tissue

Kinesiology Taping

Therapeutic elastic tape application for proprioceptive support, lymphatic drainage, and joint stability during the rehabilitation phase of injury recovery.

Rehabilitation

Therapeutic Exercise & Rehab

Structured therapeutic exercise and functional rehabilitation designed to restore strength, stability, and range of motion following musculoskeletal injury.

Diagnostic

X-Ray Digitization & AOMSI

Computerized mensuration of spinal radiographs using AMA Guides methodology — quantifying ligament laxity and generating objective impairment ratings.
