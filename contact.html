Insurance & Payment

We work with most major insurance plans and accept medical payments (MedPay) coverage for auto accident patients. Cash-pay and other arrangements are available; please call to discuss.

Please call our office to verify your specific coverage before your first visit, and bring your insurance card and a valid photo ID.

What to Bring

  • — Valid photo ID and insurance card
  • — Any prior imaging (X-rays, MRI, CT scans) on disc or as reports
  • — List of current medications and supplements
  • — Accident report or attorney contact information (if applicable)
  • — Arrive 15 minutes early for new patient paperwork
