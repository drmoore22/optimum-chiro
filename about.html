CICE — Certified Independent Chiropractic Examiner

Credentialed as a Certified Independent Chiropractic Examiner, qualifying Dr. Moore to perform objective, third-party medical-legal evaluations of spinal injury — a standard recognized in insurance, litigation, and workers' compensation contexts throughout Alabama and the Gulf Coast region.

Impairment Medicine

MACIME — Member, American College of Independent Medical Examiners

Membership in ACIME reflects ongoing participation in the scientific community responsible for advancing standards in independent medical examination, impairment rating, and causation analysis.

Spinal Imaging

CPIS — Certified in Procedural Injection & Spinal Imaging

Advanced certification in spinal imaging interpretation and procedural protocols. This credential directly supports Dr. Moore's use of Digital Motion X-Ray and advanced diagnostic imaging in the evaluation of ligamentous and disc injuries.

Dr. Moore's clinical work is informed by original research in spinal biomechanics and trauma medicine. His published work contributes to the growing body of evidence supporting objective measurement of ligamentous injury in motor vehicle accident victims.

Clinical Technology

The Region's Most Advanced
Chiropractic Instrumentation

Dr. Moore has assembled a clinical technology suite that allows for precision diagnosis and treatment unavailable at the majority of chiropractic and even orthopedic practices in the region.

Only one in Mobile, AL

Digital Motion X-Ray (DMX)

Fluoroscopic video imaging of the spine in real motion. Unlike static X-rays, Digital Motion X-Ray captures ligament instability, abnormal joint translation, and dynamic instability that is invisible on standard films — the gold standard for objectively documenting whiplash ligament injuries.

Advanced Shockwave

Duolith SD1 Ultra

Storz Medical's flagship radial and focused shockwave therapy system. Delivers high-energy acoustic pulses that stimulate cellular repair in tendons, ligaments, and chronic soft tissue injuries — clinically proven for conditions resistant to conventional care.

High-Power Laser

Aspen Class 4 Laser Therapy

Therapeutic photobiomodulation at clinical-grade power levels. Class 4 laser penetrates deeply into tissue to reduce inflammation, accelerate healing, and provide effective analgesia in acute and chronic musculoskeletal conditions.

Spinal Decompression

Computerized Spinal Decompression

Computer-controlled axial distraction therapy that creates negative intradiscal pressure, drawing herniated or bulging disc material back toward center and relieving nerve root compression. An evidence-based alternative to surgical intervention for qualifying disc pathology.

Instrument-Assisted

ArthroStim Adjusting Instrument

A precision chiropractic adjusting instrument delivering rapid, controlled impulses at 12–14 Hz — well within the body's natural neurological window for optimal joint receptor stimulation. Provides reproducible, low-force adjustment for patients who are not candidates for manual manipulation.
