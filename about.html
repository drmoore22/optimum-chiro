Clinical Technology

Advanced Diagnostic &
Treatment Technology

Dr. Moore has assembled a clinical technology suite that supports detailed diagnosis and a broad range of conservative treatment options.

Only one in Mobile, AL

Digital Motion X-Ray (DMX)

Fluoroscopic video imaging of the spine in real motion. Unlike static X-rays, Digital Motion X-Ray captures ligament instability, abnormal joint translation, and dynamic instability that is not visible on standard static films.

Advanced Shockwave

Duolith SD1 Ultra

Storz Medical's flagship radial and focused shockwave therapy system. Delivers high-energy acoustic pulses that stimulate cellular repair in tendons, ligaments, and chronic soft tissue injuries, with published evidence supporting its use in several chronic tendon conditions.

High-Power Laser

Aspen Class 4 Laser Therapy

Therapeutic photobiomodulation at clinical-grade power levels. Class 4 laser penetrates deeply into tissue and is used to help manage pain and inflammation in acute and chronic musculoskeletal conditions.

Spinal Decompression

Computerized Spinal Decompression

Computer-controlled axial distraction therapy designed to reduce intradiscal pressure and nerve root loading. A conservative option for appropriately selected patients with disc-related pain.

Instrument-Assisted

ArthroStim Adjusting Instrument

A precision chiropractic adjusting instrument delivering rapid, controlled impulses at 12–14 Hz — well within the body's natural neurological window for optimal joint receptor stimulation. Provides reproducible, low-force adjustment for patients who are not candidates for manual manipulation.
